<p><b>Noiser</b> is an unofficial Soulseek client for Android, based on Seeker, with experimental MP3 playback directly from peers.</p>
<p>Find a track in search or Browse and tap Play while the file is still arriving. Keep listening with a mini-player, background playback, and Android media controls.</p>
<p><b>Playback features:</b></p><ul>
<li>MP3 playback during peer transfers</li>
<li>In-memory folder queues, Play next, Add to queue, repeat, and autoplay</li>
<li>Embedded title, artist, album, and artwork when available</li>
<li>Seeking within audio already received and parsed</li>
<li>Notification, lock-screen, and headset play/pause controls</li>
</ul>
<p><b>Soulseek features inherited from Seeker:</b></p><ul>
<li>Search, wishlists, and filters</li>
<li>Browsing peer directories</li>
<li>Downloads, uploads, and sharing</li>
<li>UPnP port forwarding</li>
<li>Chatrooms, messages, user info, and privileges</li>
</ul>
<p><b>Development limits:</b> MP3 only, one active playback transfer, and a 256 MiB remote file limit. Peer availability and upload queues affect playback. Queues and positions are not saved after process death. External scrobblers and device-specific behavior still need testing.</p>
<p>Noiser is a separate fork of Seeker, with a modified Soulseek.NET library. Source and roadmap: <a href="https://github.com/miacadaver/Noiser">Noiser on GitHub</a>. Upstream: <a href="https://github.com/jackBonadies/SeekerAndroid">Seeker</a>.</p>
